ai agents, authored

AI agents aren't coded line by line anymore. They're authored, in plain words, on your own data.

AlohAI is a community-run training morning for Hawaiʻi's makers, small businesses, nonprofits, schools and public servants. You arrive with a job you'd like help with, and you leave with an AI agent you built yourself.

made for the islands

AI only matters here if it helps the people here. Every example at AlohAI comes from local work: the shop on the corner, the food bank on Saturday, the classroom on Monday. Pick a story to see an AI agent at work.

what you'll build with

Six building blocks, one AI agent. Pick one to watch it at work, or let the tour run. Every scene is the same volunteer AI agent from the build session.

good questions

When and where is AlohAI 2026?

Wednesday, December 23, 2026, in Honolulu. The venue and start time will be announced here soon.

When does registration open?

Soon. Seats will be limited, and the registration link will appear on this page as soon as it opens.

Who is it for?

Anyone in Hawaiʻi who wants to put AI to work: small business owners, nonprofit staff, teachers and students, public servants, IT teams and developers. No prior Copilot Studio or AI experience is needed.

Do I need to write code?

No. Authoring an AI agent starts with plain language. Developers will find plenty in the MCP, code apps and ALM parts of the morning.

What should I bring?

A laptop with a browser, and one real task you'd like an AI agent to help with. A Copilot Studio trial or a work environment you're allowed to experiment in lets you keep what you build.

Can I speak?

Please do. Lightning talks and demos about real AI agents are the heart of the morning. Submit on Sessionize.

Who organizes AlohAI?

AlohAI is run by the local community and organized by Microsoft MVP Victor Dantas. It isn't an official Microsoft event.
